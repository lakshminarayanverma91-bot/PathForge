EduAI — Internship Matcher
Generated: Today, 11:42 AM · Viability Score: 7.8/10
Problem & Solution
Problem: 4M+ Indian CS students spend weeks manually applying to internships with no intelligent matching — resulting in 90%+ application rejection rates and months of wasted effort.

Solution: AI-powered matching engine that analyzes a student's GitHub portfolio, identifies their actual skill level, and auto-matches to compatible open internship roles — then assists with one-click smart applications.
🎯 Core: GitHub → AI → Jobs
Business Model
Revenue Streams:
• Company-side: ₹15K–50K per successful hire
• Student Premium: ₹299/month (auto-apply, analytics)
• University Licensing: ₹2–5L/year per institution

Unit Economics:
Estimated CAC: ₹180 (student), ₹4,200 (company)
Target LTV: ₹2,400+ (company), ₹899 (student)
💰 LTV:CAC — 1:2.4 (solid)
Go-To-Market Strategy
Phase 1 (0–6 months): Target 5 top-tier engineering colleges. Campus ambassador program. Free for students. Partner with 20 tech startups for hiring.

Phase 2 (6–18 months): Expand to 50 colleges. Launch premium tier. Onboard 100+ companies. Begin revenue.

Phase 3 (18m+): Pan-India. International expansion (SEA). University licensing deals.
