Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions we get from students and partners.

Is Pathforge completely free to use?
+
Yes! All four core modules — CrackAI, VentureIQ, CampusPath, and MindMate — are free during our beta. We plan a freemium model in future with advanced features for power users, but the core will always remain accessible.
How does Pathforge handle my data?
+
We take privacy seriously. MindMate is 100% anonymous — no personal identifiers are stored. For other modules, session data is processed in real-time by Gemini and not retained beyond the session unless you explicitly save it to your profile.
Can my university or college partner with Pathforge?
+
Absolutely! We have partnership programmes for educational institutions including branded rollouts, placement cell integrations, and analytics dashboards. Write to us at collab@pathforge.ai with your institution details.
Which AI model powers Pathforge?
+
All modules are powered by Google Gemini — one of the most capable multimodal AI models available. We chose Gemini for its exceptional instruction-following, deep contextual understanding, and strong multilingual support for Indian students.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
+
Thank you for helping us improve! Use the contact form on this page and select "Bug Report" as the topic. Please include the module name, what you expected, and what actually happened. Screenshots are incredibly helpful too.
Is Pathforge open source?
+
Yes! Pathforge's core is open-source and available on GitHub. We believe in building in public and giving back to the developer community. Contributions, issues, and pull requests are always welcome. Check our GitHub for the repo link.
